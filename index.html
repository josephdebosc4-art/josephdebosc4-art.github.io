Clip Publisher

Clip Publisher is a personal tool used by a single content creator to publish short gaming and entertainment videos that they produce themselves to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The creator edits short vertical videos on their own server.
  2. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  3. Finished videos are uploaded to the creator's own TikTok account through the Content Posting API, either as drafts in the TikTok inbox or as posts.

The app only accesses the account of the creator who authorized it. It does not collect data from other TikTok users.

Connect your TikTok account to upload your videos as drafts.

Requested permissions: user.info.basic (your profile name and avatar), video.upload (send videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts).
