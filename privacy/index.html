Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Clip Publisher ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by an individual creator to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload and publish videos via video.upload and video.publish.

How data is used and stored

Tokens are stored on the creator's private server and are used solely to publish the creator's own videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Other users

The app does not access, collect or store data about any TikTok user other than the account owner who authorized it.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. On request, all stored data is deleted.
